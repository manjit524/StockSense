About IPO Investing

What is an IPO?

An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is when a private company sells shares to the public for the first time, enabling it to raise capital by listing on a stock exchange. Investors can subscribe during the offer window and receive shares (subject to allotment) upon listing.

How to Apply?

Apply via your broker's ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount) facility through UPI or bank mandate. Your bank blocks the bid amount until allotment — no upfront deduction. Allotment is typically lottery-based for oversubscribed retail quota.

Key Risks

IPO investing carries real risks: oversubscription doesn't guarantee listing gains, poor fundamental companies can list at a discount, and GMP (Grey Market Premium) is unofficial and unregulated. Always read the DRHP before investing.

What is GMP?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the unofficial premium at which IPO shares trade in the informal grey market before listing. It is illegal to trade in, entirely unregulated, and should NOT be relied upon as a listing price indicator. StockSense does not show GMP data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is allotment decided for retail investors?

If the retail quota is oversubscribed, SEBI mandates a computerised lottery system — every eligible applicant gets one lot if allotment percentage allows. If fully oversubscribed, some applicants get no allotment.

Can I cancel my IPO application?

Yes, you can withdraw your bid before the issue closes by contacting your broker or via the exchange's online platform. After the close date, modifications are not allowed.

What is the minimum investment for retail investors?

Retail investors must apply for at least one lot. The minimum investment = Lot Size × Upper Price Band. Most IPOs are designed so the minimum bid is under ₹15,000.

When will my account be credited if allotted?

As per SEBI's T+6 listing norms, shares are credited to your Demat account by T+2 after closure, and listing happens on T+6 from the IPO close date.

Does StockSense help me apply for the IPO?

No. StockSense is a research and analysis platform — it provides market data and AI-assisted investment shortlists. To apply for an IPO, use your broker's platform (Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, etc.) via ASBA/UPI.
