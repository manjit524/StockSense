Understanding Primary Market IPO Investing

What is an IPO?

An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is when a privately-held company sells fresh equity or promoter holdings to the public for the first time, listing its shares on public exchanges to raise growth capital.

ASBA Banking Facility

Under SEBI's ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount) system, your bid funds remain in your own bank account earning regular interest. Money is deducted only upon confirmed allotment.

Quota Allocation Rules

Mainboard IPOs allocate shares across 3 main investor categories: Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIB 50%), Non-Institutional HNI Investors (NII 15%), and Retail Individual Investors (RII 35%).

DRHP Prospectus Review

The Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) filed with SEBI discloses crucial company balance sheets, revenue growth, promoter shareholding, debt obligations, and key operational risk factors.

Frequently Asked Questions on IPOs

How is share allotment decided for oversubscribed retail bids?

When the retail portion is oversubscribed, SEBI mandates a computerized random lottery system. Every eligible retail bidder has an equal chance to receive one minimum lot if mathematically possible.

What is the minimum application amount for retail investors?

Retail investors must apply for at least one lot. The minimum application amount is calculated as: Lot Size × Upper Price Band. Most mainboard IPOs design lot sizes so the minimum bid is between ₹13,000 and ₹15,000.

What is Grey Market Premium (GMP)?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is an informal, unofficial price premium at which IPO applications trade in unofficial dealer circles prior to listing. It is completely unregulated and should not be treated as a guarantee of listing gains.

Can I apply for an IPO using UPI on discount brokers?

Yes. You can apply for any active IPO on Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, or Angel One by entering your UPI ID. Once submitted, approve the mandate notification in your UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, BHIM) before 5:00 PM on the closing date.
