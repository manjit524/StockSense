Frequently Asked Questions on ETFs

What is an ETF?

An Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is a basket of securities that tracks an index, sector, or commodity, and trades on stock exchanges like a regular share.

How are ETFs different from Mutual Funds?

ETFs trade continuously throughout market hours at live market prices, whereas Mutual Fund NAVs are calculated once per day at market close.

Why invest in Index ETFs like SPY or NIFTYBEES?

Index ETFs provide instant diversification across top companies with minimal expense ratios and lower management fees.

How do I generate a technical report for an ETF on StockSense?

Click the Report → button next to any ETF symbol to generate a full technical report with RSI, MACD, and historical return breakdowns.
