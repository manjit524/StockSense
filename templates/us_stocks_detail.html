Understanding US Equities for Global Investors

LRS $250k Annual Limit

Under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), resident Indian individuals can legally remit up to $250,000 per financial year for overseas investment in stocks, ETFs, and real estate.

Fractional Shares Investing

US exchanges support fractional investing — you don't need to buy a whole share of Berkshire Hathaway or NVIDIA. You can invest as little as $1 or $10 into any mega-cap company.

USD Currency Appreciation

Historically, the US Dollar has appreciated ~3-5% annually against the Indian Rupee, offering an embedded currency hedge that boosts overall portfolio returns for international investors.

W-8BEN & DTAA Tax Rules

Under the India-US Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA), US dividends are subject to a flat 25% withholding tax. Capital gains are not taxed in the US; they are taxed in India as LTCG/STCG.

Frequently Asked Questions on US Stocks

What are US market trading hours in Indian Standard Time (IST)?

Regular trading hours on the NYSE and NASDAQ run from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Time (ET). In Indian Standard Time (IST), this corresponds to 7:00 PM to 1:30 AM IST during Daylight Saving Time (summer) and 8:00 PM to 2:30 AM IST in winter.

Can Indian retail investors legally buy US stocks like Apple and NVIDIA?

Yes. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) permits Indian citizens to invest in US equities through the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) using registered international brokers (such as INDmoney, Vested, or Interactive Brokers).

How does StockSense AI evaluate US stocks?

StockSense utilizes a trained GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit) neural network together with 26 technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Sharpe, Beta, Max Drawdown) to classify US equities into Low, Medium, and High risk buckets tailored to your target investment horizon.

How are capital gains on US stocks taxed for Indian residents?

Under Indian tax law, holding US stocks for over 24 months qualifies as Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) taxed at 12.5% (post-Budget 2024). Holding for less than 24 months is treated as Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) and added to your slab income.
