Key Fundamentals of the Indian Equity Ecosystem

📊

NSE & BSE Bhavcopy

Bhavcopy is the official daily consolidated closing settlement file published by Indian stock exchanges. StockSense uses genuine bhavcopy historical backfills to train its GRU neural network.

⚡

Instant T+1 Settlement

India is a global pioneer in implementing T+1 settlement across all listed equities. Trades executed on Monday are settled and credited to your Demat account by Tuesday afternoon.

📑

Budget 2024 Tax Structure

Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) on listed shares held < 12 months is taxed at 20%. Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) on shares held > 12 months is taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25 Lakh exemption limit.

🛡️

Circuit Filters & ASM / GSM

SEBI and exchanges enforce daily price bands (2%, 5%, 10%, 20%) and Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM/GSM) to curb excessive speculation and safeguard retail capital.

Frequently Asked Questions on Indian Equities

What are the Indian stock market trading hours?

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) operate Monday to Friday with the following schedule:
• Pre-market: 9:00 AM – 9:08 AM IST (Order matching & opening price discovery)
• Regular Trading: 9:15 AM – 3:30 PM IST
• Post-market Closing: 3:40 PM – 4:00 PM IST

How does the StockSense GRU model predict Indian stock movements?

StockSense's GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit) deep learning model processes historical price sequences, volatility vectors, and volume patterns to output risk-stratified shortlists (Low, Medium, High risk) tailored to your specified capital and investment horizon.

What are STT and statutory charges on Indian equity delivery?

For equity delivery transactions: Securities Transaction Tax (STT) is 0.1% on buy and sell, Exchange Turnover Charges are ~0.00345%, SEBI turnover charge is ₹10 per crore, and GST is 18% on brokerage and exchange fees.
