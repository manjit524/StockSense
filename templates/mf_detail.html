Key Principles of Mutual Fund Wealth Building

Direct vs. Regular Plans

Direct mutual fund plans have zero distributor commissions, resulting in 0.5% to 1.5% lower Total Expense Ratios (TER). Over a 20-year horizon, this simple choice can add ₹15 to ₹25 Lakhs in extra wealth.

Rupee Cost Averaging (SIP)

SIP automatically acquires more units when market prices drop and fewer units when prices surge, mathematically lowering your average purchase cost without requiring you to time market tops and bottoms.

Daily NAV Cut-Off Timings

For equity funds, orders placed before 3:00 PM IST receive the same day's closing NAV. Orders placed after 3:00 PM receive the next business day's NAV. NAV is published every evening around 9:00 PM IST by AMFI.

Taxation on Mutual Funds

Equity mutual funds (>65% domestic equity) are taxed at 12.5% LTCG (>1 yr, over ₹1.25L gain) and 20% STCG (<1 yr). Debt mutual funds are taxed at your applicable income tax slab rates.

Frequently Asked Questions on Mutual Funds

How is Net Asset Value (NAV) calculated by AMFI?

NAV represents the per-unit market value of a mutual fund scheme. It is calculated at the end of each trading day by dividing the total net value of all securities in the portfolio (assets minus liabilities & expenses) by the total number of outstanding units.

What is an ELSS Tax Saver fund and what is the lock-in period?

Equity Linked Savings Schemes (ELSS) are tax-saving equity mutual funds eligible for tax deductions under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to ₹1.5 Lakh. They have a mandatory 3-year lock-in period (the shortest among all 80C instruments).

What is the difference between an ETF and an Index Mutual Fund?

Both track an underlying index like the NIFTY 50. However, ETFs trade throughout market hours on exchanges at live fluctuating prices and require a Demat account, while Index Mutual Funds are bought/sold directly with the fund house at end-of-day NAV.

How do I find my specific fund in the StockSense directory?
