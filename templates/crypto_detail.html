Understanding Crypto & Derivatives Mechanics

24/7 Global Orderbooks

Cryptocurrency markets operate around the clock with zero opening or closing bells. Liquidity is distributed globally across decentralised liquidity pools and centralized exchanges.

Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Signals

The Put-Call Ratio compares open interest of Put options to Call options. A PCR above 1.2 often signals an oversold market with potential bullish reversal, while PCR below 0.7 indicates overbought conditions.

Option Greeks & Theta Decay

Options are decaying assets governed by Delta (sensitivity to price), Gamma (rate of delta change), Theta (daily time decay), and Vega (implied volatility sensitivity).

Indian 30% VDA Tax & TDS

Under Section 115BBH of the Indian Income Tax Act, gains from Virtual Digital Assets (VDA) are taxed at a flat 30% with no loss set-off permitted, plus a 1% TDS deducted on every transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions on Crypto & Options

What is Max Pain theory in options trading?

Max Pain is the strike price where the greatest number of outstanding option contracts (calls and puts) would expire completely worthless, causing the maximum financial loss to option buyers and maximum profit to option writers (sellers).

How does the StockSense live quote engine track crypto 24/7?

StockSense integrates real-time yfinance ticker streams with automated background caching, polling live USD quotes every 15 seconds without rate limiting or server pauses.

Can I set stop-loss orders in crypto and derivative markets?

Yes. Risk management is paramount in volatile instruments. Always define strict risk parameters, position size according to available risk capital, and avoid naked option selling without hedged spreads.
