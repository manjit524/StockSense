About StockSense

Built as two honest engines,
not one black box.

StockSense runs two separate systems side by side, because a stock you'd hold for five years and a stock you'd trade in the next five minutes aren't answered by the same kind of model.

Long horizon

Multi-Exchange Advisor

A GRU neural network trained on historical closing prices across NSE, BSE, and US equities. It's a small model, trained on a sample pool rather than every ticker — treat its growth estimates as one directional signal, not a forecast to bet the house on.

Same session

Intraday Scanner

No deep model here — intraday data is too short-lived to train on reliably. Instead it reads live 5-minute candles through three established technical signals: EMA crossover, RSI, and VWAP.

What this isn't

It isn't SEBI-registered investment advice, it isn't a guarantee of returns, and it isn't a replacement for your own research. Both engines can be confidently wrong. Use them as one input among several, and size any position you wouldn't mind losing.
