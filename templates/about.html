The Architecture

The Two Honest Engines

Why we deliberately separated long-term investing from intraday quantitative execution.

Long Horizon · 1 to 10 Years Deep GRU Model

Multi-Exchange Neural Advisor

A Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) neural network trained on multi-year historical closing price sequences across NSE, BSE, and US NYSE/NASDAQ equities.

✓
Sequence & Volatility Modeling: Evaluates historical price paths and drawdown regimes to estimate multi-year return trajectories.
✓
Risk-Calibrated Bucketing: Automatically partitions asset universe into Low, Medium, and High-Risk allocations based on historical covariance and capital preservation bounds.
✓
Multi-Currency Normalization: Seamlessly balances USD and INR holdings with real-time FX conversion for unified portfolio visibility.
Transparency Note: The GRU is trained on a broad, representative asset pool rather than memorizing every individual ticker. Treat its growth estimates as a disciplined directional signal, not a guaranteed return forecast.
Same Session · 5m Candles Quantitative Pipeline

Real-Time Intraday Scanner

A deterministic quantitative signal pipeline that monitors live 5-minute candle streams and volume profiles to capture high-probability intraday momentum setups.

✓
EMA 9 / 21 Dynamic Ribbons: Identifies instant session trend direction and trend-continuation triggers with zero lagging bloat.
✓
14-Period RSI Momentum Bounds: Detects exhaustion and breakout states to protect against chasing overextended tops.
✓
Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP): Anchors intraday bias to institutional volume clusters for strict stop-loss positioning.
Transparency Note: No black-box neural networks are used here. Intraday price noise renders deep models fragile; transparent mathematical technicals provide the speed and consistency active traders need.
