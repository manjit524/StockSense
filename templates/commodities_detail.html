Key Insights into Commodities Investing

Gold as Inflation Hedge

Gold has served as a timeless store of purchasing power across millennia. Sovereign central banks and institutional investors actively accumulate gold reserves to protect against fiat currency debasement.

SGB vs. Gold ETFs vs. Physical

Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) pay a 2.5% annual coupon and offer 100% tax-free capital gains upon maturity. Gold ETFs offer instant intraday liquidity on NSE without storage risks or making charges.

Crude Oil Impact on India

India imports approximately 85% of its domestic crude oil requirement. Every $10 per barrel increase in crude oil prices expands India's Current Account Deficit (CAD) by ~0.5% and fuels domestic inflation.

Futures Expiry & Rollover

Commodities trade via standardized futures contracts with monthly expiry cycles on the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX) in India and COMEX/NYMEX globally, allowing transparent price discovery.

Frequently Asked Questions on Commodities

What is the difference between Spot Price and Futures Price?

Spot price represents the immediate cash price for delivery today. Futures price is the agreed-upon contract price for delivery at a future date, factoring in storage costs, insurance, financing interest (cost of carry), and market expectations (contango or backwardation).

What are MCX trading hours in India?

The Multi Commodity Exchange of India (MCX) operates extended trading hours from 9:00 AM to 11:30 PM IST (and up to 11:55 PM IST during US Daylight Saving Time), allowing Indian traders to participate during US market hours.

Why does the US Dollar index (DXY) move inversely to Gold?

Because gold is globally priced in US Dollars. When the US Dollar strengthens against major world currencies, gold becomes relatively more expensive for foreign buyers, dampening demand and lowering USD gold prices.

How can I invest in Gold through StockSense?

You can analyze gold price trends using StockSense technical reports, and invest through liquid Gold ETFs listed on NSE (such as GOLDBEES) or Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) via your broker.
